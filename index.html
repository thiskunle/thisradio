What an episode sounds like

  1. Part 1, 2 minutes

    Opening

    We introduce the topic and the guest.

  2. Part 2, 10–15 minutes

    The Conversation

    We explore the guest's background and experiences. Their personal stories get room to breathe.

  3. Part 3, 10–15 minutes

    Tech Meets Business

    We get into technology, business, and current ideas, from emerging tools to the decisions behind them.

  4. Part 4, 10 minutes

    The Hot Seat

    We ask the questions people often avoid and challenge assumptions, with challenging or unexpected questions.

  5. Part 5, 5 minutes

    Comic Relief

    Humour, quick-fire questions, and funny experiences. Serious when it counts, funny when it fits.

  6. Part 6, 3 minutes

    Final Word

    The guest shares one final thought.

No scripted answers. No unnecessary filters. Just conversations worth having.
